Liquid side (primary)
1

Water temperatures (T inn / T ut)

Immersion sensor or insulated clamp-on PT100 directly on pipe. T inn = cold water in, T ut = warmer water out. Avoid elbows and valves.

2

Water flow (L/s)

Ultrasonic clamp-on flow meter. Min 10 pipe diameters straight run before sensor. Formula: Q = 4.18 x V(L/s) x dT. 5% flow error = 5% Q error.

Air side (heat rejection control)
3

Ambient air (Inn) - DB + RH

Shade probe from sun. 1 m from condenser inlet, not in discharge stream. Max 60 C. This is the entering ambient air temperature.

4

Leaving air (Ut) - DB + RH

Leaving air is warmer than ambient - this is heat rejection. Expected: Q_heat = Q_water + P_el. Deviation over 10% indicates measurement error.

Balance check: Q_water and Q_air should balance within 5-8%. Larger deviation usually means flow measurement error on water side or airflow error on air side. Trust water side as primary measurement.
