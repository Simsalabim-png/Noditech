Entering air (Inn)
1

Dry-Bulb (DB)

PT100 or NTC probe, duct center, 30-50 cm from bends. Wait 3-5 min. Take 3 readings and average. Never use IR gun.

2

Relative Humidity (RH)

Calibrated capacitive hygrometer. Return air 40-60% RH. Wait 3-5 min. Cross-check with sling psychrometer if possible.

Leaving air (Ut)
3

Dry-Bulb (DB)

10-20 cm from outlet, center of airstream. Avoid condensate zone below coil. PT100 only. Wait min 5 min.

4

Relative Humidity (RH)

Supply air is 85-95% RH - hardest to measure. Allow 5-10 min stabilisation. Avoid water droplets on sensor. Best: measure WB with sling psychrometer and convert.

Airflow
5

Volume flow (m3/h)

Vane anemometer, min 5 points across grille. Multiply by free area factor (0.65-0.80). Steady-state only - system must run 10-15 min first.

Power
6

Compressor power (kW)

Clamp meter on live wire. Record kW not kVA. Steady-state only. Inverter units: average over 1 min.

Enthalpy accuracy: A 2% RH error at 90% supply RH causes approx 1.5 kJ/kg enthalpy error. With 0.2 kg/s airflow this is 0.3 kW error in Q - roughly 5-10% on a residential unit. Always allow full stabilisation and cross-check RH with wet-bulb measurement where precision matters.
